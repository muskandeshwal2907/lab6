Why mountains?

There is a point on every climb where the trees give up. Beyond it, the world is rock, wind and sky. This page is a short collection of notes on why people keep walking toward that line, and what they find when they cross it.

01

The Approach

Every summit begins in a valley. The first hours are slow, green and quiet, and they decide how the rest of the day will feel.

02

The Ridge

The trail narrows, the wind picks up, and the horizon opens on both sides. This is where most people stop talking.

03

The Descent

Coming down is the real test. Tired legs, fading light, and a view that looks completely different in reverse.
